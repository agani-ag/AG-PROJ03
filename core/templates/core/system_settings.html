Each module keeps its own rules — how many messages a day, what they say, how long anything is kept — and reads the values above when it is time to send. Change them here and every module follows from its next send.

When sending happens

No notification is pushed from a page. SyncHub's 15-minute tick does the sending for every module, and the nightly cleanup trims what has aged out — which is why a module never gets a cron URL of its own.
