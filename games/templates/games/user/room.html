{% comment %} The light every figure on this table is lit by, defined once. A moulded look is four things: the volume (a gradient with a real range, nearly white to nearly black across one form), the rim light (each shape drawn twice, the second nudged up and left, leaving a crescent of a lighter tint along the shaded side), the contact shadows, and a sphere on the end of every limb. None of it is a blur filter: a blur is the obvious way to soften a highlight and the expensive way, and with a dozen of them on a phone it is what costs the frame rate. A gradient that fades to transparent does the same for nothing. {% endcomment %}
